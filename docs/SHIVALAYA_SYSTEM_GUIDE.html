💡 Why QR Code Ordering?
Guests in luxury mountain resorts value privacy and speed. By scanning table acrylics (T-01 to T-12) or in-room standees (Rooms 101 to Villa 3), guests order directly from their own phones without waiting for a waiter to bring physical menus.
⚡ Why Live WebSockets?
Traditional restaurant POS systems require manual screen refreshing or printing delays. With Supabase real-time channels, the exact moment a guest taps "Place Order", Kundan Chef's tablet chimes and displays the ticket.
🛡️ Why Zero-Data-Loss Fallbacks?
Hill station Wi-Fi can momentarily stutter. All 3 applications feature resilient client storage (IndexedDB/session cache) and retry logic so orders are never dropped mid-transmission.

Section 3 · Guest Experience

Guest Portal: Screen-by-Screen Breakdown

What your guests experience from the moment their camera scans a QR standee.

1. Instant Context & QR Routing
When a guest scans a QR code, the URL contains a context query:
  • ?table=T-04 → Panache Dining Table 4
  • ?room=201 → Executive Room 201
Why it's there: The guest never has to manually type their room or table number. It prevents mistyped room numbers and misrouted food delivery.
2. Category & Dietary Filtering
Clear visual tabs: Starters, Panache Specials, Main Course, Tandoor & Breads, Beverages, Desserts.
Toggles for Pure Veg (🟢), Non-Veg (🔴), and Chef's Recommendations (⭐).
Why it's there: Guests with dietary restrictions can immediately filter out meat items in one tap.
3. Item Customizer & Spice Level
Guests can select portion sizes (Half / Full) and add chef notes: "Less spicy for kids" or "No garlic / no onion".
Why it's there: Eliminates back-and-forth verbal misunderstandings between waiters and chefs.
4. Cart & Transparent GST Breakdown
The floating cart drawer shows exact pricing with legal 5% GST split:
  • Item Subtotal
  • CGST (2.5%)
  • SGST (2.5%)
  • Total Payable Amount
Why it's there: Eliminates billing surprise. Full statutory compliance with Indian GST laws.
Live Progress Tracker
5. Post-Order Visual Status Timeline
After placing an order, the guest's screen transitions to the live order tracker: Received ➔ In Kitchen (Chef Preparing) ➔ Ready / On the Way ➔ Delivered.
Guests see a friendly progress bar and estimated preparation time. They don't need to repeatedly call room service or wave at busy waiters.

Section 6 · Hardware Configuration

HP Smart Tank 500 & Thermal Printer Setup

The exact one-time Chrome browser print settings for flawless invoices and KOT slips.

🖨️ Step 1: Open Chrome Print Dialog
When you click "Print Bill" or "Print KOT", Google Chrome opens the system print window (or press Ctrl + P).
⚙️ Step 2: Essential Settings Checklist
Ensure these 4 settings are configured (Chrome will remember them permanently):
  • Destination: Select HP Smart Tank 500 series (or your thermal printer).
  • Margins: Set to "None" or "Minimum".
  • Options: CHECK "Background graphics" (so logos and borders print).
  • Options: UNCHECK "Headers and footers" (removes date/URL from edges).

Section 10 · Support

Emergency Playbook & Frequently Asked Questions

Quick resolutions for the most common daily operational questions.

❓ Q: A guest scanned the QR but the menu isn't loading?
Fix: Check if the guest's phone has active 4G/5G data or is connected to the Resort Guest Wi-Fi. The portal is extremely lightweight (<2MB) and opens on any standard mobile browser (Safari, Chrome, Samsung Internet).
❓ Q: A new order arrived but the kitchen didn't hear a chime?
Fix: Modern browsers require one initial touch on the screen before playing audio. Make sure Kundan Chef taps anywhere on the screen upon morning login to allow audio alerts. Also check the tablet media volume slider.
❓ Q: A guest wants to cancel or modify an item after ordering?
Fix: Kundan Chef or Bilam Pandey can open the order card in Kitchen KDS or Reception, tap the item, and click "Cancel Item". The bill and live guest tracker update immediately.
❓ Q: How do we change prices or add new dishes?
Fix: Menu items are stored in your central cloud database (Supabase). Simply log into your Supabase Dashboard → Table Editor → menu_items to adjust any price or description in real time.
