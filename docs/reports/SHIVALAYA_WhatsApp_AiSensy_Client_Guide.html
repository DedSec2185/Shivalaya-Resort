! Why Shivalaya Resorts Must Register on Their Own

Meta (WhatsApp's parent company) strictly requires the registered WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) to be legally owned by the business entity (Shivalaya Resorts).

Why third parties cannot do this on your behalf:

1. Verification via SMS/Voice OTP: Meta delivers a one-time verification PIN directly to your official resort SIM card.
2. Legal Entity Verification: Meta requires business certificates (GST Certificate, MSME/Udyam, or Utility Bill) that match your resort's registered legal trade name and Bhimtal address.
3. Account Ownership: The verified green-tick WABA remains an asset of Shivalaya Resorts indefinitely.

1 Pre-Requisites Checklist (Before Starting)

Please gather the following items before initiating the 15-minute signup:

  • Official Resort Phone Number: Must be able to receive an instant SMS or Voice Call OTP.
  • No Active Consumer WhatsApp on this SIM: CRITICAL: If this number is currently used on a mobile phone's regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business App, open WhatsApp Settings → Account → Delete My Account before proceeding. The number cannot exist on a phone app and the Meta Cloud API simultaneously.
  • Facebook Profile with Admin Access: An active personal Facebook account that has Admin privileges on the Shivalaya Resorts Facebook Page / Meta Business Suite.
  • GST Certificate / Business Proof: Soft copy of your GST registration certificate, MSME Udyam, or Certificate of Incorporation showing the exact legal business name.
  • Address Proof: Recent electricity bill, telephone bill, or bank statement matching the address on the GST certificate.
  • Live Official Website: https://shivalayaresort.com with business contact info visible.

2 Step-by-Step Onboarding Walkthrough

STEP 2.1 Create Account on AiSensy

1. Go to https://aisensy.com and click "Start Free Trial" or "Sign Up".

2. Sign up using your official management email (e.g., frontdesk@shivalayaresort.com).

3. In the AiSensy onboarding dashboard, click "Apply for WhatsApp Business API" / "Connect WhatsApp API".

STEP 2.2 Complete Meta Embedded Signup (5 Mins)

A secure Meta (Facebook) popup window will appear:

  • Log in to Facebook: Sign in using your Facebook Admin profile.
  • Select / Create Business Account: Choose your existing Meta Business Account (e.g. Shivalaya Resorts) or create one.
  • Set WhatsApp Display Name: Enter Shivalaya Resorts (must match the branding on your website/GST).
  • Category: Select Hotel & Lodging or Travel & Transportation.
  • Website URL: Enter https://shivalayaresort.com
  • Phone Number Verification: Enter the resort phone number, choose SMS or Phone Call, and type the 6-digit OTP code received.

Click Finish. Your official phone number is now connected to the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API!

STEP 2.3 Meta Business Verification (For High Daily Message Limits)

1. Open Meta Business Settings.

2. In the left navigation, click Security Center → under Business Verification, click "Start Verification".

3. Upload your GST Certificate (as Legal Document) and Electricity Bill / Bank Statement (as Address Proof).

4. Meta reviews the documents and typically approves within 24 to 48 hours.

STEP 2.4 Retrieve API Key for Developer Team

1. In the AiSensy dashboard, navigate to Manage (left sidebar) → API Key.

2. Click "Generate API Key" and copy the alphanumeric key.

3. Send this API Key and your Registered Phone Number to our development team so we can connect your reception desk system.

Do NOT select "MARKETING". Check-in and check-out are transactional reservation alerts. Categorizing under UTILITY guarantees immediate Meta approval (often under 5 minutes), zero spam flags, and the lowest per-message cost.
